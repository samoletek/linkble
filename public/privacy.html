Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 29, 2025

1. Definitions

  • Company (referred to as "we", "us", or "our") refers to Linkble
  • Service refers to the Linkble mobile application
  • Personal Data refers to any information that relates to you
  • Account refers to your unique account created to access our Service
  • User (referred to as "you") refers to the individual using our Service
  • Event refers to activities created by users on the platform
  • Host refers to a user who creates an event

2. Introduction

Welcome to Linkble. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Linkble is a social platform that connects people through local activities and events. Given the nature of our service, we collect certain information to enable you to discover nearby events, connect with other users, and coordinate meetups.

3. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information (name, email address or phone number, date of birth)
  • Profile photo (required for account verification)
  • Your interests and activity preferences (up to 5 categories)
  • Events you create (title, description, location, date/time, category)
  • Messages sent in event group chats and direct messages
  • Join requests and event participation history
  • Reports and feedback you submit about other users or events

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device information (type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Location data (with your permission, to show nearby events)
  • App usage analytics (features used, session duration)
  • Error logs and performance data
  • IP address and general geographic location

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display events happening near your location
  • Enable you to create, join, and manage events
  • Facilitate communication between event participants through group chats
  • Enable direct messaging between users
  • Send push notifications about event updates, new messages, and join requests
  • Personalize your feed based on your selected interests
  • Process and respond to user reports and maintain community safety
  • Maintain and improve our services
  • Communicate important updates about your account

5. Location Data

Important: Linkble requires location access to function properly. We use your location to:

  • Show events within your selected radius (default 10km)
  • Display your events to nearby users
  • Calculate distances to event locations

You can adjust your search radius in the app settings. Your precise location is never shared with other users - only the event locations you create are visible.

6. Third-Party Services

We integrate with third-party services to provide our functionality:

Mapping Services

  • We use Google Maps to display event locations and enable location search
  • Your location queries are processed by Google Maps API
  • Google's Privacy Policy applies to this data processing

Authentication and Database

  • We use secure cloud infrastructure to store your account data and messages
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest

Push Notifications

  • We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications
  • Device tokens are stored securely and used only for notification delivery

Email Communications

  • We use email services to send account verification and important updates
  • We do not send marketing emails without your consent

7. Data Sharing

We limit data sharing to essential service functionality:

  • Your profile (name, photo, interests) is visible to other users
  • Events you create are visible to users within the search radius
  • Messages in event chats are visible to accepted participants
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not share your data for advertising purposes
  • We may share data only when necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect our rights

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure storage with encryption at rest
  • Row-level security policies to ensure users only access authorized data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Block other users from contacting you or seeing your events
  • Report inappropriate content or behavior
  • Opt-out of non-essential notifications
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@linkble-app.com

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Account information: Until you delete your account
  • Event data: Events are removed from public view 24 hours after they end; data retained for 90 days for safety purposes
  • Chat messages: Event chats are archived 24 hours after event ends; direct messages retained until deleted by user
  • Analytics data: 90 days in anonymized form
  • Reports: Retained for 1 year for safety and legal purposes

When you delete your account, we remove your personal data from our active databases within 30 days. Some information may be retained in encrypted backups for up to 30 additional days.

11. Tracking Technologies

We use minimal tracking technologies:

  • Essential storage: Required for app functionality and user authentication
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics to improve user experience
  • Performance monitoring: Crash reports and performance metrics

We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

12. Age Requirements

Linkble is intended for users aged 16 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 16. If you believe we have collected information from a user under 16, please contact us immediately at support@linkble-app.com

13. User Safety

We prioritize user safety in our platform:

  • Users can block other users, preventing all interaction
  • Users can report inappropriate events or behavior
  • Hosts can remove participants from their events
  • We review reports and may suspend accounts that violate our terms
  • We encourage users to meet in public places and inform friends of their plans

14. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of the app after such modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: support@linkble-app.com
