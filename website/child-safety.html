Child Safety Standards

Last updated: February 12, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Linkble is committed to the safety and protection of all users, especially minors. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. Any content or behavior that exploits or endangers children will result in immediate account termination and reporting to the appropriate authorities.

2. Age Requirements

Linkble is designed for users aged 16 years and older. During registration, users are required to provide their date of birth. Accounts that do not meet the minimum age requirement cannot be created.

3. Prevention Measures

We implement the following measures to prevent CSAE on our platform:

  • Age verification: Date of birth is required at registration and verified against the minimum age requirement of 16 years
  • Content moderation: User-generated content is subject to review and moderation
  • In-app reporting: Users can report any inappropriate content or behavior directly within the app
  • User blocking: Users can block other users to prevent any further interaction
  • Event moderation: Event hosts can remove participants from their events
  • Account suspension: Accounts that violate our policies are suspended or permanently banned

4. Reporting and Response

If you encounter any content or behavior that exploits or endangers children:

  • In-app: Use the Report feature available on user profiles and event pages to immediately flag concerning content
  • Email: Contact us directly at support@linkble-app.com

We review all reports promptly. When child safety concerns are identified, we take the following actions:

  • Immediate removal of the offending content
  • Immediate suspension of the offending account
  • Preservation of relevant data for law enforcement purposes
  • Reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other relevant authorities as required by law

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Linkble cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies and regulatory authorities in investigations related to child safety. We comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the reporting of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to regional and national authorities.

6. Data Handling for Minors

We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 16 years of age. If we become aware that a user under 16 has created an account, we will take immediate steps to delete the account and all associated data.

7. Designated Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding our child safety practices and compliance:

Email: support@linkble-app.com

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update these child safety standards as needed to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Material changes will be communicated to users through the app or our website.
